One rule that changes everything

Pieces don't stop where you tap. They slide until something stops them — or they fall off the board entirely.

  • 🚩 Slide the Battle Flag to the highlighted target cell to win.
  • 📦 Move boxes to create or block paths for the flag.
  • ⚠️ If the flag slides off the board, the game is over.
  • ↩ Undo any move, as many times as you need.
  • ⭐ Match the optimal step count for a perfect solution.
