How to Play

1

Each turn, you may slide the Battle Flag or a box one step in any direction: up, down, left, or right.

2

A sliding piece keeps moving until it hits another piece or a wall — it doesn't stop after one cell.

3

If nothing blocks it, the sliding piece exits the board and is gone for the rest of the level.

4

If the Battle Flag slides off the board, you lose. Tap Restart to try again.

5

Slide the Battle Flag onto the target cell to win. The game tells you the minimum number of steps — try to match it for a perfect solution.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does "Optimal" mean next to my step count?
Each level has a known minimum number of moves needed to reach the target. The game shows your current steps alongside that optimal number so you can see how close you are. Matching the optimal earns a "Victory ✅ — You found the optimal solution!" message.
Can I undo a move?
Yes. Tap the ↩ (undo) button in the top-right corner to step back one move at a time. You can undo as many moves as you like, all the way back to the start of the level.
The flag slid off the board — how do I continue?
If the Battle Flag exits the board, the game ends immediately. Tap Restart on the game-over screen to reset the level to its starting state.
How do I select a piece to move?
Tap any piece (the flag or a grey box) to select it. Arrow buttons will appear at the bottom of the screen. Tap an arrow to slide the selected piece in that direction. Tap an empty cell or a different piece to change your selection.
What is Level 4 / Level 9? The board looks different each time.
Level 4 is a randomly generated Easy puzzle, and Level 9 is a randomly generated Hard puzzle. Each run generates a fresh board with a guaranteed solution, so these levels are always different when you restart them after a perfect win.
Does the app require an internet connection?
No. Battle Flag is fully offline. You can play anywhere with no network required.
Does the app save my progress?
The app keeps your current in-progress game state while it's running. Full persistent save across sessions is not yet implemented — if you close and reopen the app, levels restart from the beginning.
Is Battle Flag free?
Yes, the app is completely free to download and play. There are no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or ads.
