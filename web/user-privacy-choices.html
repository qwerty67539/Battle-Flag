Deleting Your Data

Because all data is stored locally on your device (current game state only), you can remove it completely by uninstalling the App. iOS will delete all associated local storage automatically.

1

Long-press the Battle Flag app icon

On your iPhone or iPad home screen.

2

Tap "Remove App" → "Delete App"

This removes the app and all its local data from your device.

Apple Platform Choices

Apple may collect certain anonymized platform-level data (such as App Store analytics or system crash reports) independently of our App. You can manage those preferences directly in your iOS Settings:

1

Open Settings → Privacy & Security

Scroll down to "Analytics & Improvements" to control what Apple collects.

2

Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking

Manage app tracking permissions globally across all installed apps.
